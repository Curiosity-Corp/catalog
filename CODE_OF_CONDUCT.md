# Code of conduct

Curiosity Research Corporation welcomes contributions from students,
researchers, educators, maintainers, and users of every background.

We expect participants to:

- be respectful, patient, and specific;
- assume good intent while addressing impact;
- welcome different experience levels and accessibility needs;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people;
- protect private data and report security issues responsibly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct,
threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's
private information without permission.

If a problem occurs, stop engaging with the behavior and report it privately
through the security or maintainer contact route described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments,
close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action needed to
protect the community. Good-faith reports will be handled discreetly.
