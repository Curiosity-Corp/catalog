# NNNN: Short title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: maintainers who reviewed it
- Affects: variables, tags, profiles, platforms or schemas touched

## Context

What problem or pressure led to this? Who is affected (students, labs, a
specific profile)?

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences.

## Safety impact

What changes on an unrelated research machine? What is the default? What could
go wrong, and how does someone turn it off?

## Alternatives considered

What else was possible, and why was it not chosen?

## Consequences

Migration steps, tests added, documentation and changelog entries, and what
would make us revisit this.
